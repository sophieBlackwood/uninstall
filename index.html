THE ELEGANCE OF ABSENCE

Our Requiem

Over time, systems accumulate ghost processes—lingering threads of what used to be, consuming memory long after their purpose has faded. UNINSTALL was built as a sanctuary for finality.

We do not optimize for engagement or retention. We optimize for the solemn dignity of closure. By cutting persistent telemetry and letting go of digital attachments, you make space for silence.

"What is uninstalled leaves no shadow. Zero artificial intelligence. Absolute manual control."
